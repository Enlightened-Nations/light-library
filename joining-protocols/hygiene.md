# Hygiene

Hygiene is how we care for ourselves, so that we feel good and others feel good around us. We recommend it to every Light Weaver as part of being in our tribe: we meet and live close together, so this care is also a gift to everyone we share the space with.

It starts with the body and goes all the way to our energy. We look at it through the five elements.

## Body (Earth)

Our body is our first home.

**Every day**\
Shower or wash your whole body, especially armpits, feet and intimate areas\
Put on fresh underwear and socks\
Brush your teeth morning and evening, and clean your tongue\
Use a natural deodorant

**After sweating**\
After yoga, sport, work in the garden, a sweat lodge or a hot day, shower and change into fresh clothes before you join others

**Clothes and bed**\
Wash clothes after wearing them once or twice, and let them dry in the sun and fresh air\
Clothes of cotton, linen, hemp or wool let the skin breathe; synthetic fabrics hold the smell of sweat\
Change bedding every week and air your bed every morning

**Hands, hair and nails**\
Wash your hands before cooking and eating and after the toilet\
Keep hair clean and nails short and clean\
Air your shoes and wash your feet; walking barefoot on clean earth refreshes them

**From within**\
Drink enough water, eat fresh food and sleep well; a healthy body smells fresh\
Strong smells that don't go away after washing can be a sign that the body needs care; listen to it

## Emotions (Water)

Feelings flow like water. When they are allowed to move, they stay clear.

Feel what you feel, without judging it\
Let emotions move: cry, breathe, move, sing, write, walk in nature\
Before you share something heavy, ask the other if they have space for it\
Take responsibility for your feelings instead of placing them on others\
Water, a bath, the sea or a river can wash emotions too

## Actions (Fire)

What we do shows who we are.

Do what you say, and say when you can't\
Clean up after yourself in the kitchen, the bathroom and every shared space\
Leave every place as you found it, or better\
Be on time; it shows respect for the time of others\
Ask before you use what belongs to someone else

## Thoughts and Words (Air)

Thoughts become words, and words create the world around us.

**Thoughts**\
Notice your thoughts without believing every one of them\
Choose what you feed your mind: less news, noise and scrolling; more nature, silence and inspiring books\
Meditate or simply sit in silence a few minutes every day

**Words**\
Speak kindly and truthfully\
Speak to people, not about them; no gossip\
When something bothers you, say it calmly and early, to the one it concerns\
Listen fully before you answer

## Energy (Ether)

Everything we feel, think, say and do leaves a trace in our energy and in the space.

Cleanse yourself and the space with smoke from sage, copal or palo santo, with sound, prayer or fresh air\
Shake off the day before you enter your home or the sanctuary\
Rest in nature, where the earth takes what we no longer need

## With Love

Some people don't notice their own smell or the way they affect others. If you notice it in someone, tell them gently and in private, the way you would want to hear it. Caring for ourselves is a way of caring for each other.
