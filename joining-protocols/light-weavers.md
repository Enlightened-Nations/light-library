# Light Weavers

A Light Weaver brings their place, work or initiative into our circular economy and weaves it into the network. This is how we begin working with a new weaver. There are four steps, always in this order. The HerzCodex is the door. The last step happens only where it is needed.

| Step                              | What happens                                                    | Then                                                            |
| --------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1. Accept the HerzCodex           | The weaver reads and accepts our shared ethics                  | Accepted: we invite them to talk. Not accepted: we don't engage |
| 2. Receive an Invitation Talk     | We invite the weaver and get to know them and their initiative  | Both sides choose freely whether to go on                       |
| 3. Cleansing                      | We clear the weaver's field with quantum and cosmic frequencies | We look together at what has changed                            |
| 4. Realignment, Only Where Needed | Deeper work on what the cleansing showed                        | We begin working together                                       |

✧&#x20;

### Step 1: Accept the HerzCodex

Everyone who works with us lives by the same shared ethics. So this comes first, before anything else.

* **Read:** the new weaver reads the [HerzCodex](../herzcodex.md).
* **Accept:** by accepting it, the weaver commits to live and work by it.
* **Or part kindly:** if the weaver does not accept it, we don't engage. There is no judgement, and they are welcome to come back later.

✧&#x20;

### Step 2: Receive an Invitation to Talk

Our network grows by invitation: nobody joins without one. Relationship before transaction: we meet the weaver as a human being first.

* **The invitation:** once the weaver has accepted the HerzCodex, we invite them to a personal talk.
* **Getting to know each other:** who the weaver is, what their initiative does and why it matters to them.
* **Listening:** what they need, and what they bring to the community.
* **Choosing together:** both sides decide freely whether to go on.

✧&#x20;

### Step 3: Cleansing with Quantum and Cosmic Frequencies

Cleansing lets go of what is old, so the work can begin on a clear field.

* **Clearing:** we work with quantum and cosmic frequencies to release blocks and old patterns from the weaver's field.
* **Auto-Observation:** like a scientist watching what happens in a glass bottle, the weaver calmly observes how they feel during and after the cleansing.
* **Looking together:** afterwards we look together at what has changed. If the field is clear, we begin working together. If something still needs work, we go on to Step 4.

✧&#x20;

### Step 4: Realignment, Only Where Needed

Cleansing releases what is old. Realignment brings the weaver back into harmony. We offer it only where the cleansing showed it is needed, and the weaver chooses whether to take it.

* **Looking within:** the weaver observes their own emotional, mental and energetic state.
* **Realigning:** we work with frequencies to bring the weaver back into harmony.
* **Integrating:** the weaver takes time to observe what has changed and takes responsibility for it. Then we begin working together.

✧&#x20;
