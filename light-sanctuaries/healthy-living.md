# Healthy Living

## Why We Care

Many everyday things release small amounts of toxins into our bodies, our water and the land: metals, chemicals and tiny pieces of plastic. Alone they seem small, but they add up over the years.

A Light Sanctuary is a place where body, home and nature can rest. So we choose what is natural, simple and kind to the Earth.

#### Our five basic guidelines

✦ No aluminium in deodorants\
✦ No fluoride in toothpaste, drinking water, salt and more\
✦ No plastic in the kitchen where it touches heat or hot food\
✦ No Teflon or other non-stick pans\
✦ Only biodegradable, organic soaps and cleaning products

The sections below explain each one, with simple alternatives.

## Body Care

| Instead of                                           | Choose                                                                |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Deodorants with aluminium                            | Aluminium-free deodorant, crystal stone, or baking soda with coconut oil |
| Toothpaste with fluoride                             | Fluoride-free toothpaste, tooth powder or clay                        |
| Plastic toothbrushes                                 | Bamboo or wooden toothbrushes                                         |
| Soaps and shampoos with synthetic perfume or parabens | Natural, organic soaps and shampoos, solid bars without packaging     |
| Chemical sunscreen                                   | Mineral sunscreen (zinc oxide), shade and clothing                    |

## Water

✧ Filter drinking and cooking water with a filter that removes fluoride, chlorine, heavy metals and microplastics (for example reverse osmosis, then add minerals back)\
✧ Spring water is a beautiful choice when a clean source is close\
✧ Drink and store water in glass or stainless steel, never in plastic bottles left in the sun\
✧ Use a stainless steel or glass kettle instead of a plastic one

## Kitchen

Heat makes plastic and non-stick coatings release particles and chemicals into food.

| Instead of                                       | Choose                                                   |
| ------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| Teflon and other non-stick pans                  | Cast iron, stainless steel, enamel, clay or glass        |
| Plastic spoons, spatulas and ladles              | Wood, bamboo or stainless steel                          |
| Plastic cutting boards                           | Wooden cutting boards                                    |
| Plastic food boxes, especially for warm food     | Glass jars and boxes, stainless steel, beeswax wraps     |
| Aluminium foil and aluminium pots for cooking    | Baking paper without coating, lids, stainless steel      |
| Plastic tea bags and coffee capsules             | Loose tea, a metal strainer, a French press or a filter  |
| Heating food in plastic                          | Heating food only in glass, ceramic or metal             |
| Salt with added fluoride (check the label)       | Natural sea salt or rock salt without additives          |

## Cleaning

Everything we use to clean ends up in the water and the soil. We use only what nature can break down.

✧ Biodegradable, organic soap for dishes, hands and laundry\
✧ Vinegar, baking soda, citric acid and natural soap clean almost everything\
✧ Natural sponges, loofah, coconut or wooden brushes and cotton cloths instead of plastic sponges and microfibre cloths\
✧ No bleach, no chlorine products and no fabric softener\
✧ Natural laundry detergent; clothes of cotton, linen, hemp or wool shed no microplastics in the wash

## Home and Air

✧ Open the windows every day for fresh air\
✧ No sprays or plug-in air fresheners; use plants, flowers, herbs or pure essential oils\
✧ Candles of beeswax or plant wax instead of paraffin\
✧ Natural paints, oils and wood finishes when building or renovating\
✧ Bedding, towels and curtains from natural fibres\
✧ Many green plants to keep the air alive

## Garden and Food

✧ No pesticides, herbicides or chemical fertilisers; compost and natural care instead\
✧ Organic, local and seasonal food whenever possible\
✧ Fresh food instead of canned food, as many cans have a plastic lining\
✧ Grow what you can, even a few herbs on the windowsill

## Step by Step

There is no need to throw everything away at once. When something is used up or broken, replace it with a natural alternative. Little by little, the whole sanctuary becomes clean.

Gently share these guidelines with guests and offer them what they need, such as a natural soap or fluoride-free toothpaste, so everyone feels welcome.
