# Healthy Living

Many everyday things release small amounts of toxins into our bodies, our water and the land: metals, chemicals and tiny pieces of plastic. Alone they seem small, but they add up over the years.

A Light Sanctuary is a place where body, home and nature can rest. So we keep these toxins out and choose what is natural, simple and kind to the Earth.

## Metals

### Aluminium

**Found in:** deodorants, aluminium foil, aluminium pots and trays, baking powder, some medicines for the stomach, alum crystal deodorant stones\
**Choose:** aluminium-free deodorant or baking soda with coconut oil; stainless steel, cast iron, clay or glass for cooking; aluminium-free baking powder

### Lead

**Found in:** glazed clay pots and ceramics, old paint, old water pipes, lead crystal glasses, some cheap spices and eye make-up (kohl)\
**Choose:** clay and ceramics marked lead-free, plain glass, spices and cosmetics from trusted organic sources, a water filter in old buildings

### Mercury

**Found in:** large fish such as tuna, swordfish and shark, old silver (amalgam) tooth fillings, energy-saving bulbs and old thermometers\
**Choose:** small fish such as sardines and anchovies; if you replace old fillings, a dentist who removes them safely; LED bulbs; digital thermometers

## Fluoride

**Found in:** toothpaste, mouthwash, tap water in many countries, salt with added fluoride\
**Choose:** fluoride-free toothpaste, tooth powder or clay; filtered water; natural sea salt or rock salt without additives (check the label)

## Water

Tap water can carry fluoride, chlorine, metals, microplastics, pesticides and medicine residues.

**Choose:** a filter that removes all of these, for example reverse osmosis with minerals added back; spring water when a clean source is close; a shower filter for chlorine; glass or stainless steel to drink from and to store water, never plastic bottles left in the sun

## Plastics

Heat, fat and acid make plastic release more of itself into food and drink.

### Microplastics

**Found in:** plastic that touches heat or hot food (spoons, spatulas, kettles, food boxes in the microwave), plastic bottles, plastic cutting boards, tea bags and coffee capsules, takeaway cups, chewing gum, glitter, plastic sponges\
**Choose:** wood, bamboo, stainless steel, glass and ceramic in the kitchen; wooden cutting boards; loose tea and a metal strainer; your own cup and bottle; heating food only in glass, ceramic or metal

### BPA and Other Bisphenols

**Found in:** the inside lining of food and drink cans, hard plastic bottles, till receipts printed on shiny thermal paper\
**Choose:** fresh food or food in glass jars; glass or steel bottles; no paper receipt, or a digital one

### Phthalates and PVC

**Found in:** soft plastic and vinyl (shower curtains, tablecloths, floors, toys, garden hoses), and anything with "parfum" or "fragrance" on its label\
**Choose:** cotton or linen shower curtains; floors of wood, stone, cork or linoleum; toys of wood and fabric; products scented only with pure essential oils, or not at all

## Forever Chemicals (PFAS)

They never break down in nature, which is why they are called forever chemicals.

**Found in:** Teflon and other non-stick pans, waterproof and stain-resistant clothes, shoes, sofas and carpets, microwave popcorn bags, grease-proof paper and fast-food packaging, some dental floss, waterproof make-up\
**Choose:** cast iron, stainless steel, enamel, clay or glass pans; wool and waxed cotton for rain; untreated fabrics; silk or plant-based dental floss; fresh food cooked at home

## Cleaning and Fragrance

Everything we clean with ends up in the water and the soil.

### Chlorine and Bleach

**Found in:** tap water, bleach and toilet cleaners, paper whitened with chlorine (coffee filters, toilet paper, tissues, pads and tampons)\
**Choose:** a water and shower filter; unbleached paper; organic cotton pads, cloth pads or a menstrual cup

### Harsh Cleaners

**Found in:** most supermarket cleaning products, antibacterial soaps and sprays, oven and drain cleaners, dishwasher tablets\
**Choose:** biodegradable, organic soap; vinegar, baking soda and citric acid, which clean almost everything; natural sponges, loofah, coconut or wooden brushes and cotton cloths

### Synthetic Fragrance

**Found in:** air fresheners, scented candles, perfumes, laundry detergent, fabric softener, dryer sheets\
**Choose:** fresh air, flowers and herbs; pure essential oils; candles of beeswax or plant wax instead of paraffin, which is made from petroleum; fragrance-free detergent; vinegar instead of fabric softener

## Indoor Air

### Fumes and Formaldehyde

**Found in:** fresh paint and varnish, glues, new furniture of particleboard or MDF, new carpets, mothballs, dry-cleaned clothes, cigarette smoke, cooking on gas without fresh air\
**Choose:** natural paints of clay, lime or plants; natural oils and waxes for wood; solid wood furniture; airing new things outside before bringing them in; cedar or lavender instead of mothballs; open windows every day; many green plants

### Flame Retardants

**Found in:** foam mattresses, sofas and cushions, electronics, children's sleepwear\
**Choose:** mattresses and cushions of natural latex, wool, cotton or coconut fibre; dusting with a damp cloth, because these chemicals gather in house dust

### Mould

**Found in:** damp walls, bathrooms and window frames, forgotten food\
**Choose:** fresh air every day; drying wet things quickly; fixing leaks early; cleaning small spots with vinegar; help from a specialist when it spreads

## Body Care

**Found in:** many soaps, shampoos, creams and make-up (parabens, sulphates, mineral oil, synthetic colours, chemical sun filters, talc), nail polish, hair dye\
**Choose:** a few simple, natural, organic products with short ingredient lists; solid soap and shampoo bars; plant oils such as coconut, olive or jojoba; mineral sunscreen with zinc oxide, shade and clothing; henna and plant hair colours; bamboo toothbrushes

## Food

### Pesticides

**Found in:** non-organic fruit, vegetables and grains, garden sprays, insect sprays, mosquito coils and plug-ins, flea collars\
**Choose:** organic, local and seasonal food; washing fruit and vegetables well; compost and natural garden care; mosquito nets, neem and citronella

### Additives

**Found in:** packaged and ultra-processed food and drinks (artificial colours, sweeteners, flavours and preservatives), processed meat, hydrogenated fats\
**Choose:** fresh, whole food cooked at home, made of ingredients you can name; growing what you can, even a few herbs on the windowsill

## Clothes and Textiles

**Found in:** polyester, nylon and acrylic, which shed microplastics in every wash; new clothes and sheets treated with dyes, wrinkle-free finishes and anti-odour chemicals\
**Choose:** organic cotton, linen, hemp, wool and silk; washing new clothes before wearing them; second-hand clothes, which have already let go of most finishes

## Electrosmog

Not a toxin you can touch, but many sanctuaries also keep their fields calm.

**Found in:** Wi-Fi routers, phones and wireless devices, especially close to where we sleep\
**Choose:** the router off at night; phones in flight mode and away from the bed; cables instead of wireless where it is easy

## Step by Step

There is no need to throw everything away at once. When something is used up or broken, replace it with a natural alternative. Little by little, the whole sanctuary becomes clean.

Gently share these guidelines with guests and offer them what they need, such as a natural soap or fluoride-free toothpaste, so everyone feels welcome.
