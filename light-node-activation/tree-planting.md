# Tree Planting

## Purpose

Dear Guardian of the new paradigm,

Thank you for joining our synchronised global Lightseed grid.

This is a living act of connection — between you, the land, and the wider field we are co-creating.

There is no “perfect” way to do this.

Clarity, presence, and sincerity are enough.

Below is guidance for planting your tree as a conscious node in the Enlightened Nations, Oasis & Lumina Casa Lightseed Grid.

### The Heart of the Initiative

The most important element is planting your intention in your heart\
— your highest desire, prayer, and frequency for yourself and the\
Earth. This act of conscious connection, more than any procedural\
detail, creates the living link between your tree and our global grid.\
Pangaea reminds us that beneath the separation of continents,\
nations and cultures, we are one living Earth.

## Preparation

**Choose Your Tree**

✧ Select a tree species native to your bioregion

✧ Listen for which tree "calls" to you for this sacred work

✧ Prepare either seeds or a young seedling as you feel guided

**Sacred Elements (As You Feel Called)**

✧ Spiral Element: Stones arranged in a spiral pattern around the tree or placing a shell or snail house for energy amplification and cosmic order

✧ Crystal/Gemstone: To be planted with the tree roots as an amplifier (Clear quartz amplifies connection, rose quartz adds heart energy, amethyst enhances spiritual connection, or choose what calls to you) 2✧ Local Water: Collected ideally from a natural source near you and charged with your intention and blessings

✧ Personal Token: A small biodegradable item representing your commitment as guarding of the light grid (This could be a thread from your clothing, a strand of hair, a pinch of soil from your home or your saliva)

## Planting Ceremony Flow

#### **1. Arrival & Presence**

Take time to truly arrive. Feel your body, slow your breath, and settle into presence with the land. No rush. No performance. Just being here, now.

"I honor this land, its history, and all life that lives here.

I come in respect, to plant in harmony and care."

#### **2. Create Sacred Space**

In whatever way feels natural, acknowledge the four directions and invite the ancestors of the land to be present. This can be simple and authentic to your tradition.

#### **3. Synchronization with the Global Field**

Take three deep breaths, centering in your heart, and connect:

"I connect now to the heart field of Enlightened Nations and all

guardians planting trees across the Earth. As we create this living grid,

may our intentions unite as one heartbeat, one breath, one purpose. I

offer this tree as a conscious node in service to the Lightseed grid."

#### **4. The Planting**

✧ Dig the hole with conscious presence

✧ If using a crystal, place it at the bottom of the hole

✧ Plant your tree or seeds with intention and care, saying

For seedings: “I plant you as a living bridge between Earth and Sky, between worlds seen and unseen. May you serve as a conscious node in the Lightseed grid."

For seeds: "From these seeds will grow a living pillar of light, connecting the depths of Earth with the heights of Heaven."

✧ As you add soil, feel the connection forming with all other Lightseed trees globally

#### 5. Spiral Creation & Activation

Create your spiral pattern around the tree using stones or placing a shell or snailhouse activating the vortex of energy:

"With this spiral, I activate the flow of connection, drawing cosmic energy down and Earth energy up through this living vessel."

#### 6. Water Blessing & Name your Tree

Pour your blessed water onto the newly planted tree and choose its name:

"With this water of life, I bless you \_\_\_\_\_\_. May you grow strong as a guardian of this land and a transmitter in the network of light."

#### 7. Heart Connection

Place both hands on the tree (or earth above seeds) and feel the connection:

✧ Visualize light flowing from your heart into the tree\
✧ Feel this light traveling through roots, connecting with all Lightseed\
trees\
✧ Plant your deepest intention for the S Earth here\
✧ Whisper: "I recognise you as a sovereign being and commit to our\
mutual guardianship."

#### **8. Field Recognition**

Simply recognize:

✧ Others are planting across the Earth simultaneously

✧ This tree joins the living network of the Enlightened Nations & Lumina Casa field

✧ Connection requires only recognition, not effort

"This tree is part of a living network of care.

It is connected—through earth, through life, through awareness."

#### **9. Gratitude & Closure**

Offer thanks to the tree, the land, and all beings participating:

"Thank you to the land, to this tree, and to life itself. Through this living vessel, the field of the Sacred New Earth is anchored in this place."
