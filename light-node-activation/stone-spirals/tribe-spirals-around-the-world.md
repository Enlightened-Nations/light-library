# Tribe Spirals around the World

Built a spiral? Share its story and a few photos in the **Spirals and Sacred Spaces** topic of our [Telegram group](https://t.me/c/EnlightenedNations/144).

## 1. Espiral Raíz ✧ Mexico

The Root Spiral was built by **Sofía Ramírez**, together with her family and her tribe, in Primero de Mayo, Zamora, Michoacán. It was planted on 13 June 2026, next to the river where the water is born and flows, as a symbol of origin, roots and coming home.

It was started in the sowing season of corn, beans and strawberries, with the energy of the Cosmic Turtle: the one who carries the memory of her origin and finds her way by rooting into the land where she was born. Seeds of all kinds were planted in the spiral, and its centre is already growing green.

> Even if we don't know exactly where we are going, we can always remember where we come from.

#### In the builders' own words

> El 13 de junio de 2026, junto al río donde nace y fluye el agua, sembramos esta espiral como un símbolo de origen, raíz y regreso a casa.
>
> En el tiempo de siembra del maíz, frijol y fresas, dimos inicio a esta intención acompañados por la energía de la Tortuga Cósmica: aquella que lleva consigo la memoria de su origen y encuentra su camino enraizándose en la tierra que la vio nacer.
>
> Porque, aunque no sepamos exactamente hacia dónde vamos, siempre podemos recordar de dónde venimos.
>
> Esta espiral nace con la visión de volver al origen, honrar la tierra y recordar el camino de regreso a casa. 🌱🐢✨

## 2. Blumenspirale ✧ Austria

The Flower Spiral was built at the Wiccian headquarters in Freistadt, Upper Austria, by a whole family, with the youngest niece and nephew helping too. It rests between old trees, next to a small altar on a tree stump, and is decorated with flowers, feathers, shells and crystals.
