#### **The shamanic spiral requires**

✦ Minimum 4 full spiral rounds representing the 4 seasons and 4 elements\
✦ All stones must be touching each other\

✦ Add optionally earth stones from the local region\
✦ Gem stones make a spiral especially powerful. Place them in the center and/or rings, and choose according to the desired effect.&#x20;

However, rounded stones from a lake, river, or beach are always preferable.

## How to Build and Activate a Shamanic Spiral

If you feel drawn to the sacred geometry of spirals and whish to create a light portal for your family or community feel free to follow our open source guidelines below.

### Necessary Materials

| Natural stones                                   | Enough to form the complete spiral; ideally with a minimum diameter of 6m and 4 full rounds.                               |
| ------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Wooden stick and a chord                         | A chord bound on a stick to trace and measure (75 cm - 108 cm of length, depending on the spiral with you want to achieve) |
| Sage, Copal and/or Palo Santo                    | To smudge                                                                                                                  |
| Flour                                            | \~2 kg as offering during spiral activation                                                                                |
| Corn or other locally traditional grain          | \~2 kg as offering during spiral activation                                                                                |
| Offerings (flowers, corn, seeds, songs, prayers) | Leave it in exchange where stones are harvested                                                                            |
| Gem Stones (Optional)                            | Increase the spirals potential by planting quartz or gem stones of your choice in the center and/or around the spiral.     |

### STEP 1 ✧ Finding the Right Place and Asking Permission

Choose a place — a garden, inside your home, community or a personal altar.\
Ask the spirit of the place, if you can build the spiral there.\
Announce with intention what you wish to do.\
Listen carefully — you will clearly feel an unequivocal yes or no.\
Only if you perceive a yes, continue.

### STEP 2 ✧ Preparing the Space

It is essential to build in harmony with the chosen space once you have received permission.

Leave trees intact, they are elders. Some spirals include a tree within their field - that's perfectly fine. You would want to make sure tho, that you can walk besides it and it's stem is not blocking the passage to walk the spiral.

Replant small plants if possible that would be crushed by stones or people walking the spirals.

Clear the area from unnecessary items or invasive plants.&#x20;

This process often takes longer than expected, plan accordingly.

### STEP 3 ✧ Harvesting Sacred Stones

Remember: stones are living beings. They just evolve on a completly different time scale than humans do.

**Gratitude** — Bring an offering — flowers, corn or flour, your prayers and songs — to the place where you harvest the stones.\
When you find a stone you wish to include in your spiral, ask for permission and leave a portion of your offering there, if you are allowed to collect the stone.\
Thank the stone for its time in the old place. This honours the frequency it held.

You can also offer a group prayer and a gratitude song.\
Take the stones to the ritual site.

As you move them, bless their new purpose aloud. \
**"You are moving to our village to anchor a light spiral portal."**

**Don't rush** — Move slowly, intentionally. Let the stones adjust.

#### The Deep Truth

**Stones are elders.** They've been listening to the earth for millennia. When you move one, you're asking it to carry its ancestral knowledge to a new purpose. It will — but only if you ask with respect and give it time to settle.

**The telluric lines are alive.** Moving a stone that was anchoring one line and placing it to anchor a new configuration needs to be _intentional_, not accidental.

### STEP 4 — Drawing the Spiral

Draw the spiral with a wooden stick, starting from the center and marking a circle that opens outward.\
Use a second stick to accurately measure the width of each turn of the spiral. Remember: the more beautiful and regular the spiral, the better its energetic strength can develop. Even for people with a good eye, it's difficult to achieve a symetric spiral without tools.

### STEP 5 — Placing the Stones

Place the stones side by side, without gaps between them.

Before placing them, connect them to the four cardinal directions:\
Take stones in both hands and raise them upwards.\
Turn in the direction of the sun: \
East → South → West → North on the northern hemisphere\
East → North → West → South on the southern hemisphere

Then place the stone in its designated spot.\
The path of the spiral should be wide enough for a person to walk comfortably along it (Recommendation: > 75cm).\
The central circle should be large enough for a person to stand and turn on their own axis.\
In the center, instead of the first stone, you can place a female statue or a handmade womb — spiritual creativity knows no bounds.\
Honor the edge of the spiral with feminine symbols if you wish.

We recommend to place smaller stones in the inner circles and bigger stones on outer ones. A minimum of 4 full circles is ideal for a Shamanic stone spiral and reap the full benefits earlier mentioned.

Consider the final touch and building a stone door, that gets opened and closed before and after each session. This is for the vortex to fully build up with no dispersed energy while noone is walking the spiral.

At the entrance, leave a big beautiful greeting stone where people announe themselves to the spiral upon entering.

### STEP 6 — Smudging the Finished Spiral

Once constructed, smudge your spiral with sage and Copal resin, always starting from the center and working outwards.

Always walk the entire length of the spiral — never take shortcuts over the stones, as this disrupts the energy built up during its construction.\
Optional: Place a small fire in a circle to the right of the entrance. It's not required, but it provides energetic support.

### STEP 7 — The Offering of Corn and Flour

Enter the center of the spiral carrying approximately 2 kg of flour and 2 kg of corn.\
In the center: Scatter flour on the ground and place corn as an offering.\
Walk outward along the spiral step by step, continuously scattering flour and corn as you go.

These are offerings to the spirit worlds.\
When you finish, a spiral of stones, corn, and flour will lie before you.

### STEP 8 ✧ The First Spiral Walk&#x20;

Every spiral builder gets to walk the spiral for the first time, while the others hold space outside usually playing medicine music, drums, etc.

Greet the spiral at the greeting stone before entering barefoot.

While entering the spiral towards the left, let go of all the old energies and patterns you don't need any more. Connect with the spiral in the center and greet the North.&#x20;

When walking back out towards the right, this will support your manifestations and help you bring all you wish more of into your life. The spiral creates an amplified frequency for your intention. Be mindful.

After the last person walks, close the door of stones to contain the energy.

Find the full guide here: [spiral-usage-guide.md](spiral-usage-guide.md "mention")

### STEP 9 — Energetic Responsibility

Choose a name for the spiral that resonates with the space and your tribe.\
If you cannot attentively maintain the ritual space, name a guardian or return the stones to their original locations — otherwise, energetic conflicts may arise.&#x20;

If you need to dismantle the spiral: neutralise the space and leave it as you found it, or improved.\
Leave no trace, neither material nor energetic, and respect nature at all times.<br>

**Enter the spiral without shoes; her vortex creates a sacred temple.**<br>
