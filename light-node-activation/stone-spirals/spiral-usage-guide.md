## Guide for using the Shamanic Spiral

### 1. Preparation&#x20;

✧ Remove your shoes before entering (sacred space)\
✧ Enter with mindfulness and conscious intention \
✧ Temporarily move the stones guarding the entrance&#x20;

### 2. Entrance&#x20;

✧ Present yourself at the greeting stone\
✧ Walk only on the designated spiral path (no shortcuts)\
✧ Focus on what you wish to release and leave behind \
✧ Walk slowly and mindfully

### 3. Center of the Vortex&#x20;

✧ Turn once on your axis or assume a pose connecting Heaven and Earth, for example by stretching the hands and feet in a double "Y" form towards the sky an Earth \
✧ Time for prayer, meditation or setting intention \
✧ Remain in silence and reverence&#x20;

### 4. Exiting

✧ Return along the same path you entered \
✧ Do not jump or cross the rows of stones \
✧ As you exit to the right (clockwise), you express your intentions \
✧ Leave the spiral with gratitude&#x20;

### 5. Code of Conduct&#x20;

✧ Maintain a respectful attitude and silence when others are using the spiral \
✧ Do not alter the structure or move the stones \
✧ Do not bring food (except ritual offerings) \
✧ Do not interrupt ongoing rituals&#x20;
