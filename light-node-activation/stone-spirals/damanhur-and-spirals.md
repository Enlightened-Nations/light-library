## What is Damanhur?

Damanhur was born in 1975 in the foothills of the Italian Alps as a Spiritual Community founded from the inspiration of Falco Tarassaco Lava, (born Oberto Airaudi – 1950-2013). It is known around the world for its Temples of Humankind, an underground cathedral that acts as an antenna for the awakening of humanity.

Today, Damanhur is a living laboratory for the Future, a Federation of spiritual communities, and a worldwide movement that positively impacts the lives of thousands of people.

All aspects of the Damanhurian experience—from shared everyday life to its path of sacred wisdom and initiation—are aimed at the awakening of individual and collective awareness to support human spiritual evolution in an active way. You are welcome to visit!

## Why Spirals are Important

At Damanhur we believe spirals are not just shapes to be observed, but multi-dimensional or 3D structures to be walked through; not just geometric figures, but living forms. With the help of an ancient technology, called Selfica, we can concentrate inside the Spiral the flow of the universe's vital energy. Thanks to this activation they become the “nest” of cosmic energies that interact with those who walk through them. These structures harness and amplify natural energies, promoting well-being, balance of subtle bodies, and a connection to the Earth and cosmos. They serve as tools for meditation and transformation.

## Uniqueness of each Spiral

Each spiral connects with the history and the telluric lines of a place. Each spiral will develop its own purpose, that must be researched in each individual case. Some spirals for instance are specially healing, heart opening, bringing inner answers or enhance your dream work.

Damanhur asks everyone to document their spiral walks with name and the date they walked the spiral. After the first 1000 times people have entered a spiral a further activation can be performed, and a common effect can be identified by understanding peoples shared stories and experiences that are true for most people walking this one particular spiral.

## Special Guidance for Spirals

Only 1 person should be inside, when walking the spiral so the spiral can tune into the personal energy field of the walker.

In order to enhance the connection between the members of a group and their common field, a second person can enter the spiral in the moment the first person starts to walk out of the center of the spiral. The next person can enter only, once the first walker steps out from the spiral. There are never more than maximum 2 walkers in the spiral.

Walk only on the path in between the stones. When crossing over the stones, the spiral deactivates immediately. If this happens, just go out and give her some minutes to settle, before you start walking again.

For maintenance you can walk across the spiral, as you don't intend to activate her.

#### Caution with Selfica & Kids

Whilst children are welcome to help in the building of a spiral, once the spiral is activated with Selfica technology, children under 14 should not walk them anymore, because the spirals energy field is too strong and will disturb the harmonious development of their energy bodies.

Babies and infants can be taken into the spiral protected in the mother's arms. During breastfeeding the aura of mother and child is strongly connected.

We strongly advise to place a kids spiral without Selfica activation close by and explain to your kids the safe way of engaging with their own spiral. They might especially enjoy painting their kids spiral creatively.

## How to walk the spiral

1. **Set an Intention:** Reflect on your purpose for walking the spiral; it can be whatever you choose.
2. **Walk Mindfully:** Enter slowly, with deep breaths and awareness. Follow the path inward, staying present with each step, walk slowly without stopping.
3. **Pause at the Center:** Meditate or reflect in the center for a few moments, be mindful of your perceptions.
4. **Return with Awareness:** Walk back out, integrating the insights and energies gained through this experience.
5. **Before exiting:** In clockwise spirals turn around your left shoulder to go out, in anticlockwise spirals turn around your right shoulder. One step before the exit, stop briefly, and greet her.
