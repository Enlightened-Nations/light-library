## **Requirements & Benefits**

Damanhurian spirals share all benefits before mentioned and more. Each additional stone round potentialises the spiral energy field.

#### **Prepare a spiral for potentiation with Damanhur technologies**

✦ Minimum 6 full spiral rounds\
✦ Minimum 120 average steps one can take within the spiral\
✦ All stones should be ideally touching each other, and roots can also create the connection between them over time\
✦ Ideally use round water stones which are better for a harmonic flow\
✦ Add optionally gem or earth stones from the local region

For the regional earth stones a larger stone gathering radius is acceptable. Collecting them as close as possible to where the stones will be placed ensures, the telouric lines are still connected.

However, rounded stones from a lake, river, or beach are always preferable.

### Selfica Atlantean Technology

✧ Connects spirals with the synchronic lines of the universe\

✧ Selfica can optionally be activated in a later step

### Uniqueness of each Spiral

Each spiral connects with the history and the telouric lines of a place.\
It spiral will develop its own purpose, that must be researched in each individual case.\
Some spirals are specially healing, heart opening, coaching people or bringing answers.

Damanhur asks everyone to document their spiral walks with name and the date they walked the spiral. After the first 1000 times people have entered a spiral a further activation can be performed, and a common effect can be identified by understanding peoples shared stories and experiences that are true for most people walking this one particular spiral.

### Special Guidance for Spirals

Only 1 person should be inside, when walking the spiral so the spiral can harmonise with the personal energy field of the walker.\
No more then 2 people should be in the spiral at once.\
If a lot of people enter it at the same time, it won't activate its specific function.

#### Caution with Selfica & Kids

If a spiral is activated with Selfica technology, children under 14 should not walk them anymore, because the spirals energy field is too strong and could affect theirs.&#x20;

We strongly advise to place a kids spiral without Selfica activation close by and explain your kids about the safe way of engaging with their own spiral. They might especially enjoy painting their kids spiral creatively.
