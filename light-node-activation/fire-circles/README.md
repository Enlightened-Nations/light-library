# Fire Circles

### Purpose of a Fire Circle

Gathering the tribe weekly around the fire, connecting to the primary elements, through music and authentic heart connections.

**Lunada** comes from the Spanish word Luna (Moon) and expresses the **gathering under the moonlight.**

We are anchoring light in the crystalline energy grid of the Earth with every sacred fire that gets lit and every heart and soul that gets touched by it.

### Origin Story

This **tradition of Lunadas originates from the Mexican tribe in Xaman Há, Playa del Carmen.** Since around 6 years, every Sunday and Full Moon evening the tribe gathers with their instruments around a fire at a public beach for a so-called Lunada.

There are no guides, fixed schedules or costs for this family friendly event.&#x20;

The first people to arrive light the fire shortly after the sun sets. The music starts. The tribe brings the wood. The fire keeps them around until the last people leave.

The songs are mostly medicine songs, as this **circle has been seeded** by a **group of musicians and friends playing together at Avalon Riviera Maya.** But many styles of music are welcomed, as well as pure drumming beats and improvised pieces or conscious raps.

Some nights the **fire tribe** shows up and **performs their fire dance show.**

This circle has not been created by Enlightened Nations, it has been discovered by Sarah Elahina during her time in Mexico 2025, and included at the very **heart of our light node activation blueprint** due to it's incredible transformative power.

Obviously, looking back at **indigenous tribes, they all gather regularly around a fire,** we are simply adopting this in a modern culture again.

## Guidelines to seed a healthy fire circle

While there are no official rules to host a fire circle, we have developed the following **guidelines to keep the frequency** of your circle as inclusive and **high** as possible, if you decide to start one in your local community.

✧ Choose a public **place ideally in nature with easy access** to make sure no interpersonal problems inhibit someone from attending.

✧ Invite drummers and musicians to join the circle with **instruments tuned to 432 hz,** the natural Earth frequency for maximum harmony

✧ Create a **family friendly, safe space** away from environments with alcohol and substance abuse

✧ Encourage **children** to attend, they specially **love music and fire**

✧ Share the responsability for bringing wood among the tribe

✧ **Be responsible with the fire,** the last people need to put it out before leaving the space&#x20;

✧ Leave no inorganic trash and **pick up litter** before leaving the space!&#x20;

✧ Think about a possible plan B for rainy/winter days with your tribe

✧ Don't overthink it, just **make it happen.** This was birthed by a group of musician friends chilling on the beach. As easy as this.

✧ **Awoid taking ownership (Get the ego out of the way).** Nobody needs to be guiding the circle or have sole responsibility over making it happen.&#x20;

It requires a **handful of people to initiate this** for a couple of weeks/months until it establishes it's own collective dynamic. The **power of the fire and drums naturally take over** **and gather the right tribe** to keep it going.

**Enjoy the magic** that gets created and observe the unfolding **transformation** within your community ✧

### What makes a Fire Circle Sacred

Sacredness is brought into the space during preparation of the fire, by welcoming and greeting the cardinal directions, earth, sky and elemental spirits and guides.

By treating the Abuelo Fuego (Grandfather Fire) with due respect. Greet him and say goodbye in gratitude for holding space and transmuting any negative energies. A prayer and other small offerings can be offered to the fire.

You can setup a simple altar with what can be found in the space, representing the 5 elements earth, water, fire, air and ether.

Using the sacred 432 hz frequency on your instruments where it is possible to attune them.



Feel free to **share this with a visionary** in any town you wish to experience a sacred fire circle the next time you go.
