# HerzCodex ✧

The **HerzCodex** is a shared commitment to **living and working with awareness, integrity and care. I** freely choose to uphold these principles and to practise them as honestly and consistently as I can.\
\
✦ I treat myself and all living beings with dignity, compassion and respect.\
✦ I care for my physical, emotional and spiritual wellbeing, recognising that caring for myself strengthens my capacity to care for others.\
✦ I respect the natural rhythms of life and seek to live in greater harmony with the cycles of the Earth, Sun, Moon and seasons.\
✦ I recognise Earth as our shared living home and accept responsibility for protecting and regenerating her ecosystems.\
✦ I honour the Sun as the primary source of energy sustaining life on Earth.\
✦ I recognise plants and animals as essential members of the living world, worthy of care and protection.\
✦ I consider how my choices affect other people, other species, the Earth and future generations.\
✦ I act for the wellbeing of the whole wherever possible, while respecting personal sovereignty, healthy boundaries and informed consent.\
✦ I share my knowledge generously and support responsible collaboration, while respecting authorship, privacy, cultural context and legitimate protections.\
✦ I use technology consciously, transparently and responsibly, supporting connection without compromising human freedom, dignity or sovereignty.\
✦ I seek relationship before transaction, contribution before extraction, coherence before growth and stewardship before ownership.\
✦ When I make a mistake or cause harm, I take responsibility, listen, learn and do what I reasonably can to repair it.\
✦ I remain open to reflection, evidence and new understanding. I do not claim perfection; I commit to honest practice.

The HerzCodex is grounded in **Truth, Coherence, Reciprocity, Sovereignty, Transparency, Compassion and Stewardship.**

Together, we seek to create conditions in which people, communities and the living Earth can thrive — with **love, freedom, equality, responsibility and peace.**
